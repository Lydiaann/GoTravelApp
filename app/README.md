•  title and purpose of the application:

The title of the application is GoTravel!
The purpose of the application is to allow users to store their vacation details such as; locations, 
dates and times as well as setting reminders for upcoming events they have chosen. 
From one spot, users can add, delete and update as many vacations and excursions as they would like. 
There is an option for users to share their vacation and/or excursions with others. 
Setting alerts in the application allow users to be reminded of vacations or excursions coming up. 

•  directions for how to operate the application and reach all the rubric aspects:

1)  Click "Begin" from the 'main' activity to go to the 'vacations list' activity.
2)  Click the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Add sample code" to show the vacations in the database that are already created.
3)  To see vacations details, click on one of the vacations from the list in the 'vacation list' activity. This will take you to the 'vacation details and excursions' activity where all of the information is displayed.
4)  To add a new vacation, click on the orange "+" button in the bottom right corner of the 'vacation list' activity. 
5)  Fill in your vacation name and hotel information and select a start and end date using the calendar date picker.
6)  After you finish adding your vacation information, click opn the ellipsis on the top right corner to open the menu.
7)  On the menu, select "Save Vacation" to save your vacation. A message will prompt saying the vacation has been added. 
9)  To delete a vacation, click on the vacation you would like to delete. This will take you to the 'vacation details and excursions' activity.
10) Once you are on the 'vacation details and excursions' activity, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Delete Vacation" to delete your vacation. 
    A message will prompt that the vacation was deleted.
11) To set an alert for your vacation, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner on the 'vacation details and excursions' activity to open the menu and select "Notify". 
    The alert will show on the start and end dates of the vacation. 
12) To share your vacation with others, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner on the 'vacation details and excursions' activity to open the menu and select "Share Note". 
    Select how you want to share, i.e., text message, email.
14) If there is an excursion associated with that vacation, it will appear below the vacation information. To view the excursion information, 
    click on the excursion name and all of the details regarding that excursion will be displayed on the 'excursion details' activity. 
15) To add a new excursion, click the back arrow in the top left corner to return to the 'vacation details and excursions' activity and 
    select the orange "+" button on the bottom right corner. This will take you to a blank excursions page where you will fill in the blanks with your own details. 
    Select a date that is within the start and end date of the vacation it is associated with by using the calendar date picker.
16) To save the new excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Save Excursion" from the menu. A pop up message will confirm it has been saved. 
17) To share the new excursion click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Share Note" from the menu. Select how you want to share i.e., text message, email.
18) To delete the excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Delete Excursion" from the menu. A pop up message will confirm it has been deleted.
19) To add an alert for the excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Notify" from the menu. This will send a notification that the excursion is 
    starting on the date you selected.

20) To go back to a previous activity, click the back arrow on the top left corner of whichever activity you are currently on.
