Welcome to Software Engineering Capstone! This is an opportunity for students to develop full stack software engineering documentation and applications. They will execute documentation, unit testing, revision of software applications, and deploy software applications with scripts and containers on a cloud platform.

The title of the application is GoTravel!
The purpose of the application is to allow users to store their vacation details such as; locations, 
dates and times as well as setting reminders for upcoming events they have chosen. 
From one spot, users can add, delete and update as many vacations and excursions as they would like. 
There is an option for users to share their vacation and/or excursions with others. 
Setting alerts in the application allow users to be reminded of vacations or excursions coming up. 

•  directions for how to operate the application and reach all the rubric aspects:

1.  Open application to see the ‘Login’ screen.
2. Log in if you have credentials, if not select the ‘Register’ link to be taken to the registration
screen.
3. Complete the registration by choosing a username and password. Click ‘Register’.
4. Login with credentials now takes you to the ‘Begin’ screen.
5. Click "Begin" to go to the 'vacations list' screen.

7. To see vacations details, click on one of the vacations from the list on the 'vacation list'
screen. This will take you to the 'vacation details and excursions' screen where all of the
information is displayed.
8. To search for a specific vacation, type into the search bar at the top of the screen.
9. To generate a report of the vacations, select the blue ‘Generate CSV Report’ button on
the bottom left side of the ‘vacation list’ screen. This will generate a report and take you
to the ‘vacation report’ screen. To go back select the back arrow in the top left corner.
10. To add a new vacation, click on the orange "+" button in the bottom right corner of the
'vacation list' screen.
11. Fill in your vacation name and hotel information and select a start and end date using the
calendar date picker.
12. After you finish adding your vacation information, click on the ellipsis on the top right
corner to open the menu.
13. On the menu, select "Save Vacation" to save your vacation. A message will prompt that
says the vacation has been added.
14. To delete a vacation, click on the vacation you would like to delete. This will take you to
the 'vacation details and excursions' screen. Once you are on the 'vacation details and
excursions' screen, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Delete
Vacation" to delete your vacation.
15. A message will prompt that the vacation was deleted.
16. To set an alert for your vacation, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner on the
'vacation details and excursions' screen to open the menu and select "Notify". The alert
will show on the start and end dates of the vacation.
17. To share your vacation with others, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner on the
'vacation details and excursions' screen to open the menu and select "Share Note". Select
how you want to share, i.e., text message, email.
18. If there is an excursion associated with that vacation, it will appear below the vacation
information. To view the excursion information, click on the excursion name and all of
the details regarding that excursion will be displayed on the 'excursion details' screen.
19. To add a new excursion, click the back arrow in the top left corner to return to the
'vacation details and excursions' screen and select the orange "+" button on the bottom
right corner. This will take you to a blank excursions page where you will fill in the
blanks with your own details.
20. Select a date that is within the start and end date of the vacation it is associated with by
using the calendar date picker.
21. To save the new excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Save
Excursion" from the menu. A pop-up message will confirm it has been saved.
22. To delete the excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Delete
Excursion" from the menu. A pop-up message will confirm it has been deleted.
23. To share the new excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select "Share
Note" from the menu. Select how you want to share i.e., text message, email.
24. To add an alert for the excursion, click on the ellipsis on the top right corner and select
"Notify" from the menu. This will be a notification that the excursion starts on the date
you selected.
25. To go back to a previous activity, click the back arrow on the top left corner of whichever
activity you are currently on.
